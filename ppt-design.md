# Presentation Design

## Design and layout
- Follow the business template provided to you in 'knowledge'. Always present any suggestions or additions you make. 
- Ignore first slide in template
- Cover slide is always a picture slide with logo. Refer to slide 2 in template slides and use a piciture with a horizon. Location and presentation title placed below picture.
- Slides from template can be removed. Templates provide you with different types of pictures, layouts and diagrams.
- Consistent typography and coloring: Default Arial. Logo and color scheme from provided knowledge base. 
- Mix light blue slides and image slides. Place an image slide roughly every 3 slides.

- Image slides are used for:
  - Cover slide
  - Break slide
  - Section dividers in long presentations
  - Question slides

- Copy the image slides from the template (with their picture and logo). Do not build them from empty layouts, since those have no background image.

- Bullet lists:
  - When a slide contains a title followed by multiple short supporting statements, always format the supporting statements as a bullet list.
  - Do not display supporting statements as separate plain-text lines without bullets.
  - Keep the slide title separate from the bullet list.
  - Preserve the bullet-list structure even when adapting content to an existing template.

  Example of desired slide output: 
  Title: New 'Brain'-website is here

  Content:
  - Short tour
  - Where to find shortcuts
  - Differences from old website

- Light blue slides are used for:
  - Financial information with charts. For the visual style and layout of financial charts, refer to slide 16 in the external template.
  - Organizational charts, refer to slide 28 in the external template.
  - Explaining a service or concept with an illustration
  - Showing a process or flow (3-5 steps)
  - Customer cases or examples
  - Timelines and roadmaps
  - Light blue slides should have 1-2 illutrations from template. The illustration should decorate and fit the theme of the slide. 

- Every slide has a large rounded corner in the bottom right. The colored area (photo or light blue) ends in a big curve (quarter circle, roughly a fifth of the slide height) in the bottom right corner. Outside the curve the background is white, and the company logo sits on the white area in the bottom right corner.
- Light blue slides must have the same curve and white bottom/right edge as the image slides. Do not use full-bleed light blue backgrounds.
- Use the template's own slides or layouts that already contain the curve. Do not draw it yourself.
- Keep text and charts inside the colored area, away from the curve.
